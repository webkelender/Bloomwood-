Occupational Therapy

One-on-one therapy that helps children build the skills needed for everyday activities.

How it helps

  • Greater independence in daily activities
  • Better fine and gross motor coordination
  • Improved sensory processing and self-regulation
  • Stronger self-care skills

What a session may include

  • An initial assessment and goal setting with parents
  • One-on-one, play-based sessions of about 45 minutes
  • Simple activities for parents to practise at home
  • Regular progress reviews

Who it may help

Children with developmental delays, Autism, ADHD, motor or learning difficulties.

Every programme is tailored to your child. The first visit is an assessment, and Dr. Devyani will explain the plan before therapy begins.
